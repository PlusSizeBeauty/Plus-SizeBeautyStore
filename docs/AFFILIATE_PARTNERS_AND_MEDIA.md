## Per-offer information needed before publishing

- Product title and exact variant if relevant.
- Department and subcategory.
- Retailer and affiliate program/network name.
- Affiliate deep link generated by that approved program; test with its permitted method.
- Accurate short description based on verified product facts; no unsupported beauty or health claims.
- Approved product image URL/file and written confirmation that its use is allowed.
- Optional price only if the program permits it and a reliable update process exists; otherwise omit prices because they change.
- Checked date and owner/editor who verified the listing.
- Disclosure placement and any program-specific wording.

Never commit passwords, tokens, private application data, or account credentials. Affiliate tracking URLs may identify the publisher; review before committing, but public affiliate URLs are intended for public use once approved.
