About

Beauty, style, and confidence for every curve.

PlusSizeBeauty is an online editorial and affiliate project focused on fashion and beauty for plus-size women. Our categories include fashion, beauty, hair care, and skin care.

We share product recommendations and, when a verified affiliate link is available, direct readers to the retailer. We do not sell products, take orders, process payments, or handle shipping and returns. Retailers are responsible for their own products and customer service.

We aim to use clear labels and accurate information. Product links will not be published until their destination, product details, and applicable affiliate terms have been checked.
